# World Cup Escape — Player Instructions

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to World Cup Escape!

Your objective is to navigate through football-themed mazes, avoid obstacles and traps, collect goals, and reach the exit. As you progress through the stages, the challenges become more difficult.

## 2. Requirements

Before playing, make sure you have:

- A compatible desktop version of Microsoft Excel.
- The World Cup Escape game workbook.
- VBA macros enabled, if required by the game.

**Security reminder:** Only enable macros if you trust the file and its source.

## 3. Starting the Game

1. Download the official game workbook.
2. Save the workbook to a folder on your computer.
3. Open the workbook in desktop Microsoft Excel.
4. Follow any security prompts, enabling macros only if you trust the file.
5. Locate the game interface and select the Start option.
6. Wait for the maze and game information to load.
7. Check your stage, lives, score, and timer before beginning.

The exact buttons and messages may vary depending on the version.

## 4. Game Controls

Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move the player:

- **Up Arrow:** Move up.
- **Down Arrow:** Move down.
- **Left Arrow:** Move left.
- **Right Arrow:** Move right.

The controls listed above should be checked against the published workbook before release.

## 5. Objective and Scoring

Your main objectives are to:

1. Navigate through the maze.
2. Avoid obstacles and traps.
3. Collect goals to increase your score.
4. Find the exit.
5. Complete the available stages.
6. Try to achieve the highest score possible.

Check the score and goal counters during gameplay to confirm your progress.

## 6. Lives and Hazards

Be careful when moving around obstacles and traps.

Collisions cause you to lose a life and points.

Keep an eye on the Lives display. If you lose all your lives, follow the on-screen instructions to retry or restart.


## 7. Stages and Difficulty

World Cup Escape is designed around five stages, with the difficulty increasing as you progress.

Later stages are intended to challenge your navigation and decision-making skills more than the earlier stages.

## 8. Timer and High Scores

The timer allows you to monitor your gameplay time.

90 second time limit per stage.

The high-score section displays records supported by the current game version. Test whether scores are retained after closing and reopening the workbook before relying on that behavior.

## 9. Pause, Resume, Retry, and Restart

Use the relevant controls displayed in the game, if available.

- **Pause:** Temporarily stops gameplay.
- **Resume:** Continues a paused game.
- **Restart:** Attempts the current stage again.
- **Start:** Starts a new attempt or game.

Follow the messages displayed on the screen to avoid repeating actions unnecessarily.

## 10. Troubleshooting

### The game does not start

- Make sure you are using desktop Microsoft Excel.
- Check whether macros are blocked.
- Enable macros only if you trust the workbook.
- Close and reopen the workbook if necessary.

### The arrow keys do not work

- Click the game area once and try again.
- Try the on-screen arrow controls if they are available.
- Check whether another Excel cell or dialog has keyboard focus.

### The game display looks incorrect

- Close and reopen the workbook.
- Try starting a new game.
- Record the issue and take a screenshot if possible.

### The sound does not work

- Check your computer's volume and audio output.
- Confirm that sound is enabled and supported in your version of the game.

## 11. Reporting Problems

If you encounter a bug, provide your Excel version, operating system, the stage you were playing, and the steps that caused the issue.

Please do not share personal or sensitive information when reporting problems.

Have fun playing World Cup Escape!
